Programme Overview

What is TerraTern's Germany Healthcare Programme?

TerraTern's Germany Healthcare (GHC) programme prepares GNM, B.Sc/M.Sc Nursing and Physiotherapy graduates with at least 1 year of work experience to build their healthcare careers in Germany, with end-to-end support for German language training, placement assistance, visa processing and accommodation.

🩺
Open to Nursing & Physio Graduates
GNM, B.Sc Nursing, M.Sc Nursing, and Bachelor's/Master's in Physiotherapy are all eligible.
🎯
Eligibility
At least 1 year of work experience, and age between 24 and 35.
🗣️
German Language Training
Structured training to the required B1/B2 level, arranged as part of the programme (course fees apply).
🏠
Accommodation Covered
Your accommodation in Germany is covered as part of the programme.
📋
Recognition Support
End-to-end help getting your nursing qualification recognised under German licensing requirements.
🌍
PR Pathway
Skilled healthcare roles put you on a clear track towards German permanent residency.
